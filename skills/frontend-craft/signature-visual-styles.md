# signature-visual-styles — أسلوب مسمّى يبدأ منه التصميم، لا قالباً يلصق

A named style is a STARTING GRAMMAR: palette attitude + type attitude + surface behavior + motion temperament. Load this at concept time (awwwards-site-quality phase 1) to PICK a direction fast — then let the SUBJECT bend it (the bend is where originality lives; the unbent paste is where «قالب» lives). One style per project, obviously.

## The house styles (each: face, palette, surfaces, motion, when-it-fits)

1. «تحريري فاخر» LUXE EDITORIAL — the magazine.
   Ground ivory/paper (#F7F4EE-family), ink near-black, ONE deep accent (oxblood / forest / burnt sienna). Type: a real serif display (large, tight) + quiet grotesk body; huge whitespace; hairline rules and folio-style page furniture. Motion: near-still — a clip-reveal on headlines, one image parallax. WHEN: hospitality, law, fashion, heritage, anything selling trust through calm. RTL: superb fit — Arabic serif display (Amiri/Lalezar-class) with Latin captions.
2. «زجاجي داكن» DARK GLASS — the control room.
   Ground deep slate/blue-black, surfaces glass (ink@6% fills + 1px ink@20% borders + progressive-blur strips), accent one luminous hue (cyan/amber). Type: grotesk + mono for data. Motion: quick, confirmatory (glass panels slide, states glow). WHEN: dashboards, dev tools, fintech terminals. Watch: contrast floors over glass (measure per css-craft-details), and one luminous accent — not three.
3. «ورقي تقني» TECHNICAL PAPER — the blueprint/spec sheet.
   Ground light paper, ink dark, accent one working color (orange/blueprint-blue); background GRID + dotted rules; mono labels tracked-out; annotations with leader lines; stamps and measurement marks as furniture. Type: grotesk body + mono captions. Motion: minimal, mechanical (draw-on of rules and grids). WHEN: engineering, architecture, manufacturing, logistics. RTL: mirror the leader lines and margins, keep Latin-measure numerals in mono.
4. «هندسي جسور» BOLD GEOMETRIC — the poster.
   Ground flat saturated OR rich dark, huge type AS the layout (the headline occupies 60% of the viewport), shapes as composition blocks (a circle, a bar, an arc in the accent), photography cropped hard into geometry. Motion: confident and few moves (one slam-entrance, one scroll-swap). WHEN: campaigns, sports, youth brands, launches. The anti-uniform of the «warm editorial cream» default — IF the subject has the energy to deserve it.
5. «ضباب سينمائي» CINEMATIC HAZE — the mood film.
   Ground gradient dark (never flat), atmosphere layers (fog blobs, grain, light-leak accents), photography duotoned into the palette; type small and precise against big imagery. Motion: slow drift (webgl-backgrounds species), scroll = dolly. WHEN: storytellers, venues, film/music, «نريد جواً». Cost: the heaviest of the five — budgets from motion-performance apply strictly.
6. «هندسي شبكي» GRID SYSTEM — the agency index.
   Visible structure: a strict column grid with rules ON (the grid lines ship), content set inside cells like a well-set index/newspaper; heavy type top-left (RTL: top-right), meta info everywhere in mono; hover fills cells. Motion: cell-level, snappy. WHEN: agencies, portfolios, publishers, catalogs. The style that reads «منظّم بثقة» instantly.

## The choosing law

- Subject first (awwwards-site-quality phase 1): the style is the LANGUAGE the subject already speaks. مطعم موروث؟ 1. منصة مراقبة؟ 2. مصنع؟ 3. حملة إطلاق؟ 4.
- The BEND is mandatory: after picking, write the 3 divergences («تحريري فاخر، لكن أرضه بيج دافئة من حجر المطعم، وأكسنت نحاسي، والسيرِف عرض عربي ثقيل») — this line is the difference between a style and YOUR design.
- Mixing: adjacent styles may borrow ONE ingredient across (editorial type over a glass dashboard's data zone) — full mashups of 3+ styles are how «آلة جمعتني» happens.

## Done when
The chosen style is NAMED in the plan with its bend line written; the shipped page's palette/type/surface/motion all trace to that grammar; and design-critique passes on the result (a style never excuses a lens failure).
