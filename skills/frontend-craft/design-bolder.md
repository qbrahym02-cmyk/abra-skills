# design-bolder — ارفع الصوت، لا الضجيج

When a finished design is CORRECT but forgettable — «شكله عادي»، «يفتقر للشخصية»، «كل المواقع تشبهه» — do not sprinkle decorations. Boldness is AMPLIFYING the decisions already inside the design until they register. Load this when the user says اجعله أقوى/أجرأ/أكثر حضوراً — or when your critique says «صحيح لكن ممل».

## The escalation levers — pull in this order

1. TYPE MASS: the page's ONE idea deserves headline type 2–4× larger than you dared. Set the hero headline as an IMAGE (huge, tight leading, cropped by the viewport edge even) instead of a polite centered paragraph. Counter-move: everything else gets QUIETER (smaller, grayer) — boldness is the GAP, not the average.
2. CONTRAST OF SCALE: put the two most important elements at 10:1 size ratio (a giant number and a tiny caption under it). Timid designs live in a 2:1 world.
3. COLOR INTENSITY: instead of adding colors, SATURATE the ones you have — push the accent one vivid step, deepen the ground one step. A near-black #0A0A0A with one FULL-saturation accent beats five pastels every time. Check contrast still passes.
4. THE STRUCTURAL MOVE: one bold layout gesture beats ten small ones —
   - a full-bleed dark band interrupting a light page at THE story beat
   - a rotated or oversized element breaking the grid ONCE, where the meaning breaks too
   - type crossing/overlapping the hero image instead of sitting politely under it
   - a horizontal scroll shelf for a horizontally-natured subject
5. DEPTH: layered shadows or a real overlap (card casting onto card) adds physical confidence; flat-forever reads as cautious.
6. MOTION PUNCH (only if motion exists): one decisive entrance — words slam in with stagger (see text-motion-reveal), or the hero scales down from 1.05 to 1. Confidence is a 400ms decision, not a 3s parade.

## The discipline (bold ≠ noisy)

- ONE hero gesture per viewport. Two bold moves in one screen cancel each other into noise.
- The anti-stamp checklist from frontend-design still applies — bolder must not mean «cream page + bigger terracotta word» if that was the uniform you were escaping. Escalate the SUBJECT's own materials instead: the football academy gets STADIUM-condensed caps and a full-bleed pitch-green; the law firm gets a colossal serif and marble-quiet everything else.
- Every escalation must survive: «ماذا يخدم هذا؟» If the answer is «يخليه أجرأ» فقط — cut it. Boldness serves the one sentence the page exists to say.
- Re-run design-critique after: boldness that breaks alignment or contrast floors is just damage.

## Method
1. Name in one sentence the ONE thing this page must make you FEEL (دقة؟ هيبة؟ طاقة؟).
2. Choose the 2–3 levers above that most directly produce that feeling for THIS subject.
3. Apply them in code, screenshot, compare side-by-side with the previous shot (keep the old file; show the user the before/after — it sells the decision).
4. Cut anything that now competes with the new hero.

## Done when
A first-time visitor, shown the page for 2 seconds and asked «ماذا كان هذا؟», can name the subject and the feeling — and the page still passes design-critique's seven lenses.
